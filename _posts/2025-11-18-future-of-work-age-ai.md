At **WORKTECH London 2025**, I joined *The AI Workplace Challenge—Solving the Future, Live*, an interactive session hosted by the [*Work Is Weird Now*](https://www.workisweirdnowpod.com/) podcast. The panel debated real workplace challenges and developed recommendations with the audience.

This is the framework I brought to the discussion.

## 1. What is the most important consideration for the future workplace in the age of AI?

**Task redesign, not job replacement.**

AI does not replace roles neatly. It reshapes the *task mix*. The organisations that thrive will:

- Map which tasks AI can perform reliably.
- Identify the human tasks that become more valuable.
- Continuously **rebundle work** as AI evolves.

This avoids the twin traps of **over-automation**, which can damage trust and capability, and **under-automation**, which leaves large productivity gains unrealised. [DeepFlow](https://www.deepflow.com/) is designed to help organisations see and redesign work at this level.

## 2. What is the ideal role for humans in a largely AI-operated workplace?

**Humans become the sense-makers, standard-setters and accountability layer.**

We contribute:

- **Direction-setting:** goals, values and definitions of “good”.
- **Judgment:** ambiguity, trade-offs and nuance.
- **Relationship work:** trust, empathy and conflict resolution.
- **Integration:** connecting decisions across contexts and time.

**AI does the doing; humans do the deciding.** Our value shifts from execution towards interpretation and alignment.

## 3. How should HR and talent leaders prepare people for this future?

Build a **continuous learning and adaptation system**, not a one-off training course. That means:

- **AI fluency for everyone.**
- **Regular role and task audits** to guide upskilling and workforce design.
- **Durable human capabilities** such as judgment, communication and problem-framing.
- **Psychological safety** so people can experiment confidently with AI.

If HR can operationalise continuous adaptation, the workforce can remain relevant even as AI accelerates.
